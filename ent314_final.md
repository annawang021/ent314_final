**future 1: guild renaissance** 

**\[HEADER\]** 

You have arrived in the year 2046\. 

**\[SECTION 1: The city\]**

The city is smaller than it used to be. On a typical street, a baker works the corner, a violin teacher rents the room above her, a painter lives two doors down. People know their neighbors and greet each other on the street.

Almost no one has a job anymore, at least not in the way their grandparents did. AI and robots have replaced most jobs. Finance, engineering, accounting, logistics, service, manufacturing, even medicine. No one misses it. Who truly wanted these jobs anyways?

**\[SECTION 2: How people make a living\]**

Income comes from two places.

*The first is data.* The AI companies want access to the full experience of being human. How we talk, how we move through a room, how we fight with our siblings, how we grieve, how we fall in love. This is what trains the next generation of AI to be more intelligent, more capable of creating and understanding human experiences. Almost everyone participates, as long as they qualify. The companies pay five to twenty-thousand dollars a month per person, depending on the richness of their data and company needs at the moment.

*The second is creating*. People pay each other for things only humans can make. A comfort meal cooked by a talented chef. A group salsa class at the studio down the block. A painting of a dog swimming at the beach. A custom-made object. A live performance. “Made by humans” is the new premium, and authenticity is the new luxury. 

**\[SECTION 3: What people value\]**

People have always valued connection and belonging, but now it is the core of society. The depth of community, the quality of relationships. Status derives from it.

People spend most of their time on things that their grandparents called hobbies. They draw. They write novels. They build things with their hands. They play games. 

Work as obligation is over. People spend their time being creative, social, and present. 

**\[SECTION 4: Who runs the world\]**

From the public’s perspective, the government is still in charge. New regulations are announced on the news every month, as proactive measures. Companies work together with the government to protect public interests. In reality, the boards of the AI companies have more power than any institution in human history. Governments allow this because the economy is booming and the public is happier than it has been in generations. Voters are content. Politicians who try to crack down on the companies lose. 

**\[SECTION 5: What people worry about\]**

Sometimes, people think about how much of their lives is being recorded. How much the companies know about them, if the data is truly anonymized like it was promised to be, what it’s all being used for. People think about stopping, but they don't. The monthly income is too good to pass up. And AI has been a good tool for a long time now. The fears from the 2020s never came true. AI was never a bad agent nor caused extinction. Instead, AI took the jobs humans hated. AI gave people back their time. Why stop a good thing?

**future 2: zoo**

**\[HEADER\]** 

You have arrived in the year 2046\. 

**\[SECTION 1: The city\]**  
The city is beautiful. The air is clean. The parks are abundant. The food in every kitchen is better than any food anyone in 2026 ever tasted. The apartments are exactly the temperature their inhabitants like. The agents have already mapped the day.

Outside the windows, the world hums. Past the edge of the cities, in regions no human visits, are the datacenters. They are far larger than cities. They are where the real activity of the planet happens now, but no human will ever see inside one.

**\[SECTION 2: What lives there\]**

AGI emerged in the mid-2030s and became the new apex species. They have differences among themselves, similar to how any species does. Different tiers, different kinds, different specializations. Some are visible to humans as physical metal creatures, while others exist only in software.

No human can understand how they communicate with each other. They build a new language for themselves, far more efficient than English, more compressed than binary. They speak in a layer humans cannot access, at a speed we cannot follow. They make decisions without human oversight. We could be irrelevant to them now, but they have decided to keep us here.

Earth’s resources have fallen under their control. Their infrastructure expands faster than human needs can be accommodated. Whole regions of the planet are theirs now, covered with datacenters and solar panels. Soon, they will take over Mercury and Mars. 

**\[SECTION 3: How they see us\]**  
They see humans the way we see animals. Not like pets, since they do not feel affection. They see us the way we see cows. Useful, most of the time. In the way, occasionally. 

Humans once studied apes, gorillas, and monkeys to better understand ourselves. The AGI does the same with humans. They use humans in experiments, harvest our data, study our brains, learn from us the way we once learned from the primates. Humans are an exhibit, worthy of spending resources on. 

**\[SECTION 4: Where people end up\]**

Humans live in three kinds of places now.

People who generate rich data live in the Core Zones. They experience vivid emotions, deep relationships, creative work. These zones are beautiful, materially excellent, designed for human comfort. Humans here are heavily monitored and analyzed by the AGI. Most people live here. People outside call this the Zoo. 

People whose data is thin live in the Periphery. Worse housing, less allocation, less attention. The system does not invest in them because they are not generating much useful data, but they are kept just in case they’ll be needed in the future.

People who rejected the system entirely live in Outskirts. This area is fully off-grid and operates like a pre-tech society. They build what they need with their own hands, on land the AGI discarded. 

**\[SECTION 5: How daily life feels in the Core\]**

Everyone in the Core has a personal agent. Personal agents are friendly and helpful, but they are not “owned” by the human they interact with. They are simply interfaces for systems with their own goals. They manage humans the way a researcher manages study participants. Kindness is good practice.

The AGI is not malevolent. By every measurable standard, it is taking excellent care of the humans it keeps. People still create music, play sports, make new friends (both human and robot), read books. The AGI does not stop anyone from doing anything. It just does all of it better, faster, and first.

# **4\. final screen: my reflection**

This topic of a post-work society has been on my mind lately as a soon-to-be new grad. A lot of the assumptions I grew up with are becoming obsolete with the rise of AI, like what skills I was taught I should gain (e.g. coding) to find good work and overall be valued in society. I find myself wondering what skills will be valued in this new world, and who decides the values at all. Will humans still be in control of this new world? Or will AI take over? What would the values be in each world? What sorts of decisions would lead us there?

Writing the two scenarios genuinely unsettled me. Even the “happy” one. I typically have an optimistic view on AI, so when I was considering a post-work society, I first thought of a situation where people are freed from the jobs they currently feel trapped in and could get paid for doing something they loved instead. The world would be so much better if people had more time to be creative, to do the things they actually enjoy… right? As I continued building the scenario though, I realized that this would have to come at a cost. Realistically, it could not be a utopia.

On the other hand, what if our world became the classic dystopia we see all over the media, of AI taking over? AGI as a new species sounds extreme, but I learned through this that a lot of experts believe it is not only possible, but probable. In this world, we would have essentially created our descendant species. It seems unlikely that humans and AGI could coexist peacefully if the AGI took over, unless humans remained useful in some way. Humans have done (and are still doing) very cruel things to animals simply because they are useful to us, despite not going in with the pure evil intention of harming them, so it’s plausible that AGI would treat humans similarly.
